Privacy Policy

Fire TV Cameras is a private app used by one household to view its own Nest cameras on its own Fire TV. Last updated October 29, 2026.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google's Smart Device Management API to list the owner's Nest cameras and play their live video. It also receives doorbell press alerts through Google Cloud Pub/Sub so it can show the front-door camera.

What the app stores

The app stores only the sign-in token Google issues, on the owner's own Fire TV. It does not record, save or upload video, images or audio. Live video goes directly from Google to the TV.

Sharing

The app does not share any data with anyone. It has no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in their Google account under Security, Third-party connections, or in the Google Home app under partner connections.
